Here's an excerpt from a kbb.com review of a 96 Kia Sephia by user Kia Owner on 5/15/2008:

> "This car got me through college which is more than I expected of it."

And the user Sugar Pie said in 2007:

> "There is a nice "ora" around this car and it's one of the best cars I've seen."

Though, the user Grams wasn't so impressed. She said on 2/15/2008,

> "The body is CHEAP, any little bump in a parking lot will crease it, and it is very noticible...I WON'T EVEN LET MY GRANDCHILDREN, RIDE IN THIS CAR. My next car will be MADE IN AMERICA!!!!"

I've been in a pensive daze over the last couple weeks, as one finds themselves after crossing the spine of a decade. How do I characterize these last thirty years and how do I want the next thirty to look? I think I will do a review of my twenties over the next few weeks. I like my little corner of life with my wife and my dogs and my Scarlett 18i20 Focusrite, but I don't know when or how we got here. I feel like a pinball that's just as surprised to still be bumping around as the drunk guy spamming the flippers. I feel like a 96 Kia Sephia winking at the Waymo in the next lane.

Anyways, I just wanted to pop in on the blog. It's been collecting dust for a few weeks. I find that the routine of this site is helpful for my wellbeing.

Here's another excerpt from a review. This one from user tfaulk13 on 10/28/2011 in Bakersfield, CA:

> "It just blows me away, how people can expect a vehicle to " magically" last for 200,000 miles or more without performing the recommended services at the recommended intervals. If people would just perform these services as scheduled, their vehicles would easily last beyond 250,000 miles. I have a 1981 Toyota Corolla that has been properly maintained and it has over 420,000 miles on it. It still has good compression and great performance...not to mention it gets about 27 miles per gallon in town driving."

I've found that memoirish writing is a sort of recommended service for a properly maintained Tyler. Many cars have quirks that you learn to work around. Like, the user Red Dog said in 2010 about the 96 Kia Sephia,

> "...It is quite uncomfortable to get in and out of but then again that might be the fact that Im 5'8" and weight 275lbs. My dashboard did turn brown an curl upwards, this is a common problem with these cars. Engine is a bit noisy."

Thirty years into owning this body, I could tell you its quirks. I could rattle off the pinch points of maintaining this machine as quickly as I could tell you how to light a cigarette. It eats up oil. Use sun covers or the dashboard will sour. He needs to write shit down.
