### 100 Workouts

<figure class="blog-media blog-media--portrait">
  <img src="img/2026-summer-post-mortem/gym.jpeg" alt="Gym progress photo">
</figure>

I reached 100 workouts over the summer. As of right now, I'm at 116 workouts. Last year, I worked out 77 times. That was my last personal record. I'm on pace to hit 150 by the end of the year, or maybe even 200 if I really go for it.

My body is changing pretty quickly. It's often that I tell Lindsey, while flexing hard enough to make my face the color of a coca-cola can, "You see that? That's new! That there is a rear delt. Yep. And those are my lats. You ever seen anything like that?"

Some cool achievements I've had across this process:

- I maxed out the seated row machine (250lbs)
- I benched 225
- I did my first pull up
- I leg pressed 500lbs
- I bought these pants from ebay last year and they were so small on me that I was under no delusion of ever being able to wear them, but I forgot to sell them, and now I fit in them comfortably.

I don't have a specific long term goal. I like the way it feels to get stronger in the gym. I like the way it feels to see my body transform. I'm just gonna keep enjoying that and let the short term goals kind of steer the course. My next short term goals are 150 workouts before the end of the year and 250lbs on the bench press.

I do want to say that I'm on testosterone and GLP. I am working incredibly hard to take care of my body, but if reading any of this makes you feel insecure about your own goals and work, know that I am also getting some assistance from these drugs. I don't think it discredits my work, but I know it'd be helpful for me to read this caveat. We're unfortunately in another fatphobic cultural moment, and even in my little baby blog I don't want to be a part of perpetuating any of that shit. There's so many cool things about taking care of your body that have nothing to do with being not fat.
