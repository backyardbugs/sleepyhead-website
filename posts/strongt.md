### Testosterone
Every time I’ve shared that I’ve begun taking testosterone, it feels like I’m sharing a secret. I’ve encountered more charged reactions than I expected. Friends have said things like, “Wow, good for you, that’s a huge choice,” “Wait really? You’re on T currently?”, “Who is your doctor? Have you considered getting a second opinion?”

I don’t mind these reactions. I’m just as fascinated by this medicine and how it’s impacting my body. But I find it strange that I feel some tension in sharing it. Maybe it’s something that I’m imposing on other people and they don’t actually feel any type of way. It’s hard to put in words, but it does feel like a slight faux pas to share that I’m on T.

Regardless, I’ve been on T now for four weeks. I get my first bloodwork follow up in two more weeks. I was put on T because my level was a very low 210 and my family history supports this as a genetic thing that has been treated for other family members via T shots.

I have a lot of thoughts on testosterone, but for now I’ll just say: I haven’t felt this good this consistently in a very very long time. I feel sharper than I ever have. I’m getting better sleep. I’m improving in the gym. My depression, anxiety, and adhd have all improved. The once a week shots in my ass cheek are not ideal, but I’m loving how I feel so much that I am growing to get excited for the next shot. If I feel this good after 4 shots, how good will I feel after a year of them? Lindsey is a super hero and has been poking me. I am so grateful. The shot is long and pointy and it was scary the first time. But it’s fine now. I don’t love it, but it’s over quickly. I also don’t love that I will likely be on T shots for the rest of my life, but I’d rather this than feeling like shit for the rest of my life.

It is not lost on me that there are many trans people who are unable to afford or are not in safe environments where they can take TRT or HRT. I am grateful that it was so easy for me to receive gender affirming care as a cis man, and I have more respect for my trans male friends that have been taking these shots every week. I don’t think I’d ever thought about the practical tediousness and pain of the shots themselves before needing them myself.

### High Press
We posted a teaser of a cover of a Noah Kahan song ([High Press on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DU9MoXHif0r/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==)) and it went semi viral, getting three hundred thousand views and several thousand shares. It’s exciting to get attention for something we made. But admittedly, it’s thrown us off track. We’ve been in the trenches trying to hastily write, record, mix, and release a full version of the cover, in hopes that we can capitalize on this momentum.

But what we really want to be doing is working on our new music. The Kahan side quest has stalled the momentum we had built in our season of song writing. We’re wrapping up the cover, so hopefully this side quest will be over soon and we can get back to what we’re supposed to be working on.

Who knows, maybe the side quest will be worth it. If we suddenly got 100,000 monthly listeners on a cover, that’d be a great way to on ramp the release of our new music. And if not, then at least we learned a lesson on the frivolity of going viral on instagram.

### Julep

Lindsey and I released the new single, [Waterworks](https://open.spotify.com/track/57y2rVhmyHkko7tRQ3nc4g?si=a96a87ac89b44af5). I love this song. It’s got some really cool production elements that I wouldn’t have known to do a year ago. It’s just little things like saturating the top end of the kick with FabFilter’s Volcano, or making a texture by processing a jungle beat sample through tremolo and a phaser, but it’s exciting to know that I’m growing as a songwriter and a producer.

So far, the song has 200 listens. That’s not much, of course. And really, we don’t care either way. I mean, we do—like if it suddenly got a million listens we’d be incredibly happy—but we make music because it helps us understand ourselves and because it’s fun and because we’re good at it and it’s satisfying to watch ourselves get better each time. We have and would make music that only we hear. All that being said, numbers wise, it’s the best release we’ve had since we released I Miss Halloween.
