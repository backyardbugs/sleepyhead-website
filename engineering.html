Audio Engineering

Hey I'm Tyler! Remote-friendly mixing, mastering, and production help for bands and solo artists. Florida-based. If you're local I can connect you with a homie's studio for tracking. I do offer tracking if your budget isn't compatible with the studio, but my gear won't be of the same caliber as tracking in a professional studio. My strengths are in mixing and production. Hire me and I'll make your song sound dynamic and interesting and professional. If you listen to the portfolio below you'll hear professional mixes I've done and I hope you will see the variety of creative production choices spanning the songs. To me, that's the most exciting part of mixing and mastering, where you take a song and elevate through a compelling production. I'm inspired by producers like Jack Antonoff (Bleachers, Taylor Swift), Eric Valentine (Third Eye Blind, Taking Back Sunday, The Story So Far), Justin Vernon (Bon Iver), and Will Putney (Norma Jean, Knocked Loose).
